Controllers & pause

In comics, press the Architect button or Explorer joystick button to continue. Move the joystick up to open this menu. In play, the Architect selects and toggles passages; the Explorer moves and uses the joystick click to pause or pick orchard fruit.

During the orchard, press both buttons together to pause. Move the joystick up/down to choose a menu item, left/right to adjust a setting, then press either button to confirm.

Connect each R3 to the laptop by USB using its existing test sketch. Select the corresponding serial port below. The Main ZIP is an LCD/buzzer test and does not relay these inputs.
