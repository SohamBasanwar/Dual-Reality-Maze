Interior and future options

Full-size breadboards

All three reserve 166 × 55 × 10 mm. Battery doors are independent of the electronics lids. Internal cradles, straps and clips keep the assembly screwless.

Optional ESP32

Teal/blue reference blocks show the optional module and jumper space. The 60 × 32 mm board allowance stays movable until the exact board is selected. A breadboard-mounted board still needs antenna-clearance and wiring checks.

R3 or R4 in Main

The common UNO cradle and rear sliding opening accommodate a board change without replacing the whole shell. The shutter opens approximately 35 mm; verify the selected USB-B/C cable and any short extension.

Single-colour manufacture

Bronze-coloured insulating polymer, engraved grip texture and raised role motifs. A separate translucent diffuser carries the RGB status light.

Before large prints

Use the seven fit-test pieces for battery access, shell latches and the cable shutter. Preserve the curved shape and use accessible supports where needed. The final UIC machine/material profile controls the print settings.
