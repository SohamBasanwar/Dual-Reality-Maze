Controller features, labelled

Reveal sensor: raised and isolated

The photoresistor is above the case roof inside a removable half-dome. An opaque floor separates it from the electronics cavity; two narrow passages carry its leads. The centre-face location requires a deliberate reach away from the joystick, grips and rear tether.

Printed membrane is a test, not guaranteed translucent. Try the supplied 0.4 / 0.6 / 0.8 mm roof variants in the chosen material, or use the alternate clear-film frame. Test covered/uncovered readings while cycling the internal RGB LED. Seal around the leads if light leaks through their small bores.

Architect: gate drive exposed for explanation

The removable guard is omitted only in this detail view. The assembled previews include it.

How the gate moves

The supplied servo horn turns a pin. The pin slides sideways in the horizontal slot while lifting the gate in its two vertical guides. The fixed bridge keeps the horn hub seated; a small C keeper retains the drive pin. The exact horn interface remains provisional.

Try the motion

Nominal crank radius: 7 mm
Nominal travel: 12.1 mm
Proposed sweep: −60° to +60°

Centre the servo before installing the horn. Establish actual endpoints slowly on a bench; the software must stop before a mechanical jam.

Screwless retention: splines transmit torque; the frame bridge prevents axial withdrawal. The pin’s head and rear keeper keep it in the horn and slot. Their fit and durability are unverified.

What to verify before a full print

Start with the grip/battery sample, a shutter track and plate, carrier receivers and actual components. Check insertion, release, cable bends and repeated opening. Exact hole-based pins remain deferred; the current carriers retain the boards by their edges.

A 30 cm cable includes its internal service loops, so the distance between boxes will be shorter. Keep shutters open while connected and use the internal cleats for a soft bundle restraint.
