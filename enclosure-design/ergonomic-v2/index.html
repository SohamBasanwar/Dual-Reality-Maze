One press powers the system

A crystal cap operates a separate momentary button connected to a latching electronic power switch. The external regulated supply branches to all three boards and the servo. Main coordinates data; its Arduino does not carry the other devices' power.

Regulated DC supply → protected latching power switch → star distribution
                            ↑ crystal pushbutton          ├ Main USB-C
                                                          ├ Explorer USB-B
                                                          ├ Architect USB-B
                                                          └ Servo branch
Wired SDA / SCL / common ground connect the boards.
Main built-in Wi-Fi connects to the laptop.

Basic latching power needs no firmware. The reference switch is Pololu 2808 LV; final module, current budget, cable and protection selection remain open. Combined power does not run through breadboard jumpers.

Large breadboards change the proportions

Each controller allows a 166 × 55 × 10 mm breadboard body plus wiring space. The nominal shell is 240 × 183 × 54 mm; Main is 240 × 180 × 66 mm. The guarded Architect mechanism adds about 27 mm above its lid. The lower grip ends are about 63 mm wide; test reach and holding comfort before printing the full set.

Printing and finish

Use a single filament color, broad tactile runes, and one-color character decals on flat areas. Fine character details suit stickers or an approved engraved plaque. Locating rails and a lid skirt are modeled; fitted retaining keys and the servo horn keeper still need completion.

Engineering Makerspace publishes a 290 × 290 × 285 mm Voron envelope and accepts STL in mm. The College of Education ETL service has separate eligibility/approval requirements and accepts STL only. It does not publish enough machine detail to assume the same fit.

Code status

The game and two-port USB input adapter exist. Editable enclosure code is included. The R3-to-Main I²C protocol, Main Wi-Fi bridge, and game-synchronized LCD/servo firmware remain unfinished. Existing Arduino ZIPs are component tests. No firmware was uploaded.
