Fade control and wiring

A nonblocking four-second fade controls all four green LEDs together through one suitable MOSFET. Each LED has its own resistor. The RGB status LED keeps its own circuit. Choose a verified unused PWM pin; the helper has not been integrated into or flashed to the game firmware.

02 / Adjust. Insert the key. Check the fit.

The fixed corner locates the board. Two jaws set its length and breadth. Pull a locking key sideways, move its jaw, then align and fully reinsert the key. The key carries the sliding load; avoid squeezing the electronics.

The recessed + ↔ − symbols indicate a larger or smaller opening. The nominal setting is the largest front-to-back setting in the handheld bases: the battery bay and adjacent board limit further growth. Main and the control carriers have room on both sides of nominal.

Indexed settings are 3 mm apart. Small nonconductive fit pads may be needed between settings. They must touch safe edges, not solder joints or electronic parts. Open the jaws for installation; only use a position with a complete locking-key passage.

A thicker insert supports a thinner board under the fixed upper lips; a thinner insert accommodates a thicker board. Check that the display, joystick and connectors still align.

Material-separated layouts for UIC review

The installed set is separated into 88 opaque structural parts and 7 translucent inserts. Print the fit samples first. Each provisional plate contains one selected material and colour, with allowances for brims and supports.

1 · Fit samples

Rail, jaw and key; one lighting coupon with its matching bezel and diffuser. Check actual components and material before the full shells.

2 · Opaque parts

Bronze is the preferred look; UIC lists black as a stock option. Separate large shell jobs from trials, then fill compatible spare space with smaller parts.

3 · Translucent inserts

Three emblem diffusers plus four Main accents. Neutral translucent material can serve all seven; the LEDs provide their colours. No special purchased lenses are required if UIC can print them.

4 · Provisional Voron layouts

Layouts use the published 290 × 290 × 285 mm Voron volume. Staff must confirm usable exclusions, orientation and supports. The model-only 3MF files preserve separate parts and do not contain G-code.

UIC publishes a 0.6 mm nozzle / 0.3 mm layers and recommends 0.5 mm horizontal / 1.0 mm vertical separation. Several current prototype fits are finer. Request a small-parts review and test coupons; do not submit the entire set as print-ready.
