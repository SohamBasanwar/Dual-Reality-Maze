Measure before printing

Nominal controller shells: 214 × 138 × 54 mm. Main: 160 × 120 × 48 mm. The layout assumes an unverified half breadboard. Exact holders, closure keys, connector openings and the screwless servo-horn keeper need physical measurements and fit tests. These are CAD studies, not final print releases.

The full crests suit printing and engraving. Fine vinyl requires a weed test. Through-cut stencils need bridges or a carrier. Use the SVG artwork layer for marking and keep the technical paper templates separate.

Wired I²C connects both R3s to Main. All boards use USB power; Main uses built-in Wi-Fi for laptop data. Servo power needs its own regulated support. The communication firmware remains a separate integration task.

Original visual references: existing Dual-Reality Maze game art. Three one-ink designs, supplied in black and white. Engineering Makerspace in RRB confirmed. No print submission.
