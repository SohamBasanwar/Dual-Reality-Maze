A gate that fits a handheld

The motor is concealed inside the Architect. A rotating disk changes the arched window from bars to an open passage, with a nominal 110° sweep. The cover rises 6.8 mm above the lid, compared with approximately 61 mm for the previous tower.

A clear PET viewing shield is recommended before handling the moving mechanism. The CAD review leaves the viewport open to expose the disk. Calibrate limits with the actual servo and fitted horn; the visual change is a physical gate-state indicator, not a functioning miniature passage.

The door has a rounded exposed perimeter and a broader rolled seating transition. Two leading tabs and a release catch retain it. The name sits on the inside of the door, away from the palm. Physical grip and latch coupons are still essential.

Your name and initials
