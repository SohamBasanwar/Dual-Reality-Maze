Dual-Reality Maze

Revision 08 · recessed castle gate, contoured battery access and personalized parts.

Actual CAD renders. Prototype fit, printed latch strength, light transmission and powered operation remain unverified.

The castle gate returns

The arched portcullis and visible lifting motion are back. Its drive now sits inside the controller. The guard reaches 40 mm above the 58 mm lid plane, compared with 60 mm for the earlier guard: a 20 mm reduction. The gate travels about 12.12 mm through its nominal range.

The closed and open views use actual CAD parts and their calculated travel. Horn fit, tiny pin durability, printed latch strength, pinch access and loaded servo operation still need physical tests. The flat rotary-window concept remains available in Revision 07 for comparison.

Controller and Main views

Battery access and internal layout

The raised service pad has been removed. The battery door is a 2.4 mm vertical skin shaped from the original oval underside and set 0.15 mm inward. Its two leading tabs and release catch remain. Physical grip and latch coupons are still essential.

Your name and initials

Large parts carry “Soham Basanwar”; selected small parts carry “SB”. Thin keepers, the guard and tiny fit-sensitive parts remain unmarked. Every printed part is labelled with your full name in its filename, CAD object label and parts list.
