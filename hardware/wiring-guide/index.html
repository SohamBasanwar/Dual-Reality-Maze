Sources and component assumptions

  • Arduino UNO R3 official full pinout
  • Arduino UNO R4 WiFi official full pinout
  • Arduino UNO R4 WiFi GPIO and Qwiic limits

Read against the current Architect, Explorer, Main and Protocol.h files. The LED drawing assumes a bare common-cathode RGB. The buzzer drawing assumes a small passive piezo. A button uses INPUT_PULLUP (D2 to GND), not the old pull-down circuit. The LDR is bare with a 10k resistor, not an AO/DO sensor module.

The main LCD and both controllers share SDA/SCL. Arduino R3 A4 is SDA and A5 is SCL. Main uses the 5V header bus; its Qwiic connector is a separate 3.3V bus and is not used. Pin-label order on modules, RGB LEDs and MOSFET packages must be checked for the actual part.

Assembly order: local GND and low-current components; signal wires; servo supply connections with common ground; interboard GND then SDA/SCL; inspect for shorts; power all boards together; connect the game. Do not connect or remove jumper wires while powered.

The diagrams give electrical connections, not exact breadboard hole coordinates. Five holes in a terminal strip are connected; the opposite side of the center trench is separate. Long power rails may be split halfway. Use continuity checks and jumpers for intended joins.
