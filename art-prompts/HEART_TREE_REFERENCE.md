# Chapter III — the Dryad's Heart Tree

Original game design, October 19, 2026. Generated with the built-in image tool.

## Reference research

- [World-Tree Project: The World-Tree in Literature](https://www.worldtreeproject.org/exhibits/show/yggdrasill/the-world-tree-in-literature): a living tree whose roots and branches connect realms. This informed the rooted, sheltering silhouette and memory-bearing canopy.
- [Jordan Van Der Merwe: Plod world-tree and mother-tree concepts](https://jordankvdm.artstation.com/projects/rJzAZE): a monumental mother tree used as a central game landmark. This informed the landmark role, not a copied design.

The game asset is original: an emerald crown with ivory blossoms, golden hanging leaves, intertwined trunks and a luminous leaf-shaped heart. It replaces the ordinary tree beside the Dryad. Roots remain grounded, actors render in front, and the canopy fades locally for player visibility. Gentle gold light, drifting leaves and a subtle canopy sway identify the living sanctuary.

## Exact generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: isolated transparent fantasy game environment sprite. Reference image is ONLY a style guide for detailed dimensional pixel art rainforest game. Create ONE original sacred Heart Tree, a special ancient world-tree inhabited by a female Dryad, clearly more majestic than the ordinary trees in the reference. Tall full silhouette, broad tiered emerald and deep teal canopy with luminous ivory blossoms and small golden leaf lanterns hanging on vines, thick elegantly intertwined ancient brown trunks framing a radiant amber-gold leaf-shaped heart in a natural hollow. Deep moss and subtle golden sap veins in bark, spreading grounded buttress roots, a small inviting arched hollow under the heart. Three-quarter elevated front game view matching the reference, rich pixel clusters and readable strong shape. Crown about 1.1 times tree height in width, trunk and roots occupy lower half, full tree visible with transparent margins. No people, no text, no labels, no square tile, no floating island, no scenery background. True transparent alpha, no cast shadow outside the roots. The warm heart and white blossoms distinguish it from ordinary green forest trees.
