# Title screen artwork

Created using the built-in image-generation tool. Saved in images/title-screen.png and maze-source/work/assets/title-screen.png, and embedded in the standalone HTML.

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a spectacular premium fantasy pixel-art GAME TITLE SCREEN background for Dual-Reality Maze, landscape 16:9 composition, no lettering, no logo, no UI. Reference1 is existing Explorer sprite: preserve cream clothes, pale-trim leather cap, brown backpack and boots. Reference2 is the character sheet: use only the brown-skinned adult Architect with dark beard AND mustache, navy-purple gold-trimmed robes, turquoise clasp and scroll/tool belt; do not include Princess or ghosts from the sheet as foreground characters. Reference3 prologue establishes rich pixel-art palette and world. Composition intentionally reserves LEFT 45 percent and upper-left as spacious dark midnight-blue mist and stars with subtle ruins, to place a large gold game title and menu in HTML. On RIGHT half, Explorer and Architect stand together on a dramatic purple stone bridge leading to a giant ornate cyan-violet swirling wormhole arch. Architect raises a hand with violet geometric magic, Explorer carries a warm lantern. Behind them extends a vast floating labyrinth of purple stone over a blue luminous abyss, tiny orange torches, crystals and hanging vines, distant castle silhouette beneath a crescent moon. Strong foreground silhouettes, cinematic perspective, rich crisp pixel clusters, luminous magical details, elegant royal-fantasy atmosphere, inviting adventure. Keep important characters and portal within central 80 percent vertically so cropping to 16:9 does not cut them. No extra foreground people, no modern objects, no text, no split panels. Finished game key art, consistent recognizable characters.
